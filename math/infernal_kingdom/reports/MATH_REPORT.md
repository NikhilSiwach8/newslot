## Maximum-win verification

- Highest payout in every mode's book set: base 66,666.00x, base_soul 66,666.00x, hellfire 66,666.00x, covenant_blood 66,666.00x, covenant_soul 66,666.00x, abyssal 66,666.00x, furnace_blood 66,666.00x, furnace_soul 66,666.00x. None exceeds 66,666x.
- The cap is enforced in `GameExecutables.pay()` for the whole round; `tests/test_rules.py` plays every mode against an artificial 300x cap and checks that payouts stop exactly at the cap, nothing pays after the `wincap` event, and base + bonus wins equal the round payout.
- Max-win books come from the `wincap` criteria (WCAP strip mixed into bonus play); they follow the same rules and reach the cap through normal payouts.
